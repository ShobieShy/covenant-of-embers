# Act II story brief

Approved by ShobieShy on 4 Nov 2026. The implementation plan is [PLAN_0.7.0_ACT2.md](PLAN_0.7.0_ACT2.md).

**Act II: The Overgrown Deep** — level name **The Blightcoil**.

Below the Warrens the Labyrinth opens into a jungle that should not exist underground: a vast circular cavern where the **Rotheart**, a dying world-tree corrupted by the Breach, has pushed its roots through three tiers. At the bottom, **the Sump**, poison pools in green mire. Above it **the Canopy Ring**, a spiral of root bridges and moss ledges, coils around the trunk. At the top, **the Crown**, the rot has bloomed into fungal growths that exhale drifting spore fog. The goblins who fled down here are gone, leaving husks sprouting spore-caps, and the **Mossbound** — rotting treant wardens who once protected the tree — strangle anything that comes close.

The party learns Crazy Joe passed through first: his ritual marks are carved into the bark, and he has been feeding the Rotheart Breach-fire to speed the rot. The **Verdant Mother**, a dryad spirit bound to the tree, begs them to burn out the infection before it climbs higher into the Labyrinth. At the Crown they face the **Blightmaw Matriarch**, a huge spore beast fused to the tree's corrupted heart. When she falls, the Rotheart dies, the poison fog clears, and the way opens down to the Drowned Forge. Beneath the Matriarch: an empty cocoon, torn open from the inside — something the Verdant Mother never mentioned.

Design asks from ShobieShy: poison level, corrupted jungle, circular level layout, 2-3 levels of verticality.
