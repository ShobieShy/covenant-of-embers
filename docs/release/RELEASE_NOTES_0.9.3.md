A floor under every level.

**What's new since 0.9.2-prealpha**
- If you fall off the map, you skip the downed window and come back at the last waystone you touched.
- If you have not touched a waystone yet, you come back at the level spawn.
- You keep your gold, your loot, and your story.
- In co-op, only the person who fell is moved. The host checks the fall.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1).

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- The halls are still grey boxes. The larger town, landfill, and Association grounds are the next versions.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
