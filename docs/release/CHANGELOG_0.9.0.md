# Changelog: 0.9.0-prealpha (2026-11-11)

Act IV, The Sunken Ruins. SaveGame stays on version 1.
The in-game build string is `0.9.0-prealpha`. The menu name is Pre-Alpha 0.9, because a `.0` patch is not shown.

## 0.9.0

- Five floors under the Slagworks, checkpoints 14 to 18. The door after Forgemaw opens them.
- The halls are flooded. Standing in the water spends Breath. When Breath is empty, the water costs health. An air bell on each floor refills Breath.
- Drowned cultists on the earlier floors. The Drowned Choir on the nave. It fights surfaced, then submerged.
- After the Choir falls, the coral sarcophagus is there. It is empty. The nave pad is the way home.
- Loot from these floors is Water, at item levels 36, 38, 40, 42, and 46.
- This is not Breach fallout. Old Oak is still Old Oak. The Landfill is still the tutorial. Act I is still the Goblin Warrens.

## Open story questions

- The hymn text, and the cult's name for Joe, are not written.
- Hymn-shards are not items yet.
- The Choir is one body with two phases, not a crowd of singers. How those bodies should look is open.
- What, if anything, was inside the sarcophagus is open. The prop is an empty cracked shell.
