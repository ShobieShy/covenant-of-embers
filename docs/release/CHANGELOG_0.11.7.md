# Changelog: 0.11.7-prealpha

The in-game build string is `0.11.7-prealpha`. The menu name is Pre-Alpha 0.11.7.

## 0.11.7

The quest marker and the minimap pin follow the main trail from a new game through Act V. Each step stays on a real node in the right zone, on a surface you can stand on. The Filth and Joe's Trash mark the north ledge before they spawn. The Warden mark stands on the dais. A labyrinth step follows the floor you are on. Act I starts when the Warden falls, so the labyrinth door is marked before you walk through it. That door stands on the Breach's south slab.

The release export keeps the terrain textures. Town, the Association, and the Landfill no longer fall back to the checkerboard when a layer list is packed.

Act V's Inverted Spire is dressed: a false sky, the inverted tower, rock shelves, crystal bridges, and the arena ring. Checkpoints 19-23 keep their stairs, wind, and the Warden's floor.

The Landfill is the painted junk pit. Bob's lab is the painted two-floor hollow.

0.11.6 is in this build and was not tagged on its own. Vendors can sell the backpack, including Sell all and Sell all junk, and a slot can be marked junk or favorite.

The Core stays closed. Music stays silent. Save version stays 1. Co-op protocol stays 7.
