# Changelog: 0.8.7-prealpha (2026-11-11)

Everything since 0.8.6-prealpha. SaveGame stays on version 1.
The in-game build string is `0.8.7-prealpha`.

## 0.8.7

- A shield replaces the shield's capacity, regen delay, and effect. Taking it off restores the old numbers. It is not a second bar.
- Shield effects are ward (faster regen), spike (damage while the shield holds), and calm (a shorter delay).
- A flask on G. One charge. It heals and does not drink a potion. It refills at a rest and at the Mage Association.
- A relic: Ember Chip, Ward Bead, or Sip Stone. The effect is gone when the relic comes off.
- Two more bases, Cinder Staff and Mire Wand, and two parts, Slag Grip and Mire Core. Placeholder art.
- Those extras can drop from a side stream that does not change the kill roll. The rate is unset (0) until it is tuned.

## Still not in

- Covenant Escalation as a playable dial. The code is still `Vigil`.
- The Abyss and Endless Descent.
- Act IV, The Sunken Ruins.
