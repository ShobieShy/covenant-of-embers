# Changelog: 0.9.3-prealpha (2026-11-13)

A kill plane under every level. SaveGame stays on version 1.
The in-game build string is `0.9.3-prealpha`. The menu name is Pre-Alpha 0.9.3.

## 0.9.3

- Falling more than 8 m under the lowest floor skips the downed window and sends you back.
- You come back at the last waystone you stood at, or at the level spawn if you have not touched one.
- Gold, loot, and story stay. Health, shield, mana, and Breath refill.
- In co-op only the player who fell moves. The host checks the fall and picks a point on a real marker.
- Standing in the Landfill bowl, or on any floor that is still part of the level, does not count as a fall.
