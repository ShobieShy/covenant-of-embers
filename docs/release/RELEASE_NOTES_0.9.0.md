Act IV, The Sunken Ruins.

**What's new since 0.8.9-prealpha**
- The door after Forgemaw opens five flooded floors.
- The water spends your Breath. If Breath runs out, it starts taking health. Each floor has an air bell that refills Breath.
- Drowned cultists wait in the halls. The Drowned Choir is on the last floor. It fights above the water, then goes under. That second phase is the water rising.
- When the Choir falls, a coral sarcophagus is in the nave. It is empty. The pad there is the way home.
- Loot in this act is Water, a step above the Slagworks.
- This is not a Breach-fallout act. Old Oak is still Old Oak.

**Open story questions**
- The hymns, and what the cult called Joe, are not written.
- Hymn-shards are not items in this build.
- The Choir is one body in two phases. Whether it should be several singers is open.
- What was in the sarcophagus, if anything, is open. You only see the cracked shell.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1).

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- Staff sounds, Bob's look, and the scavenger's lines are placeholders.
- The halls are grey boxes.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
