Covenant Escalation, after Forgemaw.

**What's new since 0.8.7-prealpha**
- After Forgemaw, Covenant Escalation can be set from 0 to 10. In the code it is still Vigil. Tier 0 leaves the story run alone.
- Higher tiers make enemies tougher and shift the loot weights. A floor you have not cleared cannot be remixed.
- Town Square and Old Oak have a chest, a vendor, and a slot machine. The chest takes a breach key, or gold if you have no key.
- Bounties are repeatable and pay gold.
- A mantle hangs on your shoulders. It does not change your stats.
- Enemies bark when they chase you, when they are hit, and when they die.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1). The tier is optional and starts at 0.

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- Staff sounds, Bob's look, and the scavenger's lines are placeholders.
- The door after Forgemaw does not open the next act.
- The Abyss and Endless Descent are not a mode you can walk yet.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
