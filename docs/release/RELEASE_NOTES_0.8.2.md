Act III is in. The Slagworks sits under the Blightcoil: floors 9 to 13, ending
on Forgemaw.

**What's new**
- The Slagworks, floors 9 to 13. Forgemaw on the last floor. Waystones take you
  home.
- After the Matriarch, the Crown door opens the way down.
- Lava, heat, carts, and falls cost health only.
- A new game starts in the Mage Association. Town Square is a walled square
  again, and the vendors, the doors, and the Flagon work.
- The Landfill is a trash valley you walk north: the dump, the compactor, the
  rot, the Filth, then the road to the Breach. Beating the Filth opens that road
  and starts the Breach quest.
- The Landfill is named the Landfill.
- The wounded scavenger can be talked to. Moss beds lie flat. Bob looks like a
  worn wizard. Staff sounds are tones. Those four are still placeholders.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1).

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- Staff sounds, Bob's look, and the scavenger's lines are placeholders.
- The Matriarch has very little health (authored at 120), and her Death
  animation does not play out.
- The door after Forgemaw does not open the next act.
- Acts II and III are not yet one walk through the Breach. Later acts are not
  in.
- Items do not come to you from a distance, crouch-jumping onto ledges is not
  in, and enemy attacks are still simple.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit.
  Your progress is already saved.
- Some of the Blightcoil is still grey boxes: slabs, the shrine, chests, and
  fog. The Slagworks is grey boxes too.
