Shields, a flask, and a relic.

**What's new since 0.8.6-prealpha**
- A dropped shield replaces how much shield you have, how long it waits to recharge, and one effect. Ward recharges faster. Spike adds damage while the shield holds. Calm shortens the wait. Taking the shield off puts the old numbers back.
- Press G for a flask. It has one charge, it heals, and it does not drink a health or mana potion. It refills when you rest and when you return to the Mage Association.
- A relic is a short effect you can take off: Ember Chip, Ward Bead, or Sip Stone.
- Cinder Staff, Mire Wand, Slag Grip, and Mire Core are in. The art is still a placeholder.
- The extra gear can drop without changing other loot rolls. The rate is not tuned yet, so a normal kill does not add one until that number is set.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1).

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- Staff sounds, Bob's look, and the scavenger's lines are placeholders.
- The door after Forgemaw does not open the next act.
- Covenant Escalation, the Abyss, and Endless Descent are not playable yet.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
