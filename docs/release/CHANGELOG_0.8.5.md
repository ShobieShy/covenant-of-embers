# Changelog: 0.8.5-prealpha (2026-11-11)

Everything since 0.8.2-prealpha that is on master. SaveGame stays on version 1.
The in-game build string is `0.8.5-prealpha`.

## 0.8.3

- Ranged loot walks to you.
- Crouch-jump onto a ledge.
- Enemy combat styles. Attacks are no longer one pattern.

## 0.8.4

- Second Wind, including solo. A kill while you are down revives you.
- A compare card for the item under the crosshair.
- Four wieldable slots. Keys 3 and 4 draw the extra two.
- Consecrations can roll on ordinary Rare+ drops. The pool is larger.

## 0.8.5

- Named uniques from the Filth, the Blightmaw Matriarch, and Forgemaw. Repeat kills can drop them.
- Named rare enemies in the Landfill, the Act II cavern, and the Slagworks, each with a unique of their own.
- Elites and named rares show a name, a glow, and a health bar.
- Named uniques use a pearlescent color on the loot beam and the compare card.
- Accessibility settings, saved in the settings file, not the save: colorblind tints and shape marks, subtitle size, background, and speaker names, UI text scale, shake and bob sliders, flash reduction, a voice slider, rebinding, toggle versus hold for crouch, sprint, and aim, crosshair size and color, and high-contrast outlines.

## Still not in

- 0.8.6 action skill.
- Covenant Escalation as a playable dial. The code is still `Vigil`.
- The Abyss and Endless Descent.
- Act IV.
