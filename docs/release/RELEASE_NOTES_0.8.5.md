Named uniques, named rares, and accessibility settings.

**What's new since 0.8.2-prealpha**
- Loot on the ground can walk to you. You can crouch-jump onto a ledge. Enemies fight in more than one style.
- Second Wind, including when you are alone. A compare card for the item under the crosshair. Four weapon slots. Consecrations on ordinary Rare+ drops.
- The Filth, the Blightmaw Matriarch, and Forgemaw can drop a named unique on a repeat kill.
- The Landfill, the Blightcoil, and the Slagworks can spawn a named rare with its own unique.
- Elites and those rares show a name, a glow, and a health bar.
- Accessibility is in Settings: colorblind tints with shape marks, subtitles, shake and bob, flash reduction, a voice slider, rebinding, crouch / sprint / aim toggle, crosshair size and color, and high-contrast outlines.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1).

**Known issues**
- Music is silent.
- Staff sounds, Bob's look, and the scavenger's lines are placeholders.
- The door after Forgemaw does not open the next act.
- Covenant Escalation, the Abyss, and Endless Descent are not playable yet.
- In co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
