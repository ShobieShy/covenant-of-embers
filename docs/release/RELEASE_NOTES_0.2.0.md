# Covenant of Embers 0.2.0-prealpha: The Open World

Eldoria is one connected world now. Walk from Old Oak through Town Square, down into the
Landfill and on to the Breach with no level loads. The ground changes as you go: grass gives
way to mud, then to rubble, and the ash thickens at the Breach.

**What's new**
- One continuous world with streaming. Doors into the Mage Association and the Breach rooms
  are the only load fades.
- Waystone fast travel, a world map with fog of war, and a minimap.
- The whole intro plays out in the open world.
- Co-op works in the open world. Stay within 400 m of the host, and you'll come along when
  the host fast travels.

**Your saves.** Saves from 0.1.x carry over. You'll pick up in the world near where you left off.

**Prefer the old flow?** Launch with `--classic-levels`.

**Known issues**
- Performance hasn't been checked on integrated GPUs or the Steam Deck yet. That testing is deferred.
- Road and ridge transition strips are not in 0.2.0.
- In co-op, the joining game can close with an error code on exit. Your progress is already saved.
