Below the Breach the Labyrinth opens. Act I, the Goblin Warrens: go down through the crater
gate, fight through four floors of goblin-held rooms and face the Goblin King on floor 5.

**What's new**
- The Labyrinth, Act I: four generated Warrens floors (the same layout each time you come
  back on a save) and the Goblin King's throne hall.
- Rest shrines on every floor: heal, save, set your respawn and craft.
- The Goblin King: rock throws, ground slams and a roar that calls in grunts.
- Better loot the deeper you go, secret-room chests and bigger drops with more players.
- Co-op: the party follows the host down the stairs.

**Fixed**
- Leaving the Breach rooms no longer drops you through the ground outside.
- Co-op: a teammate in your line of fire no longer soaks your bolts (only Light bolts stop
  on a teammate, to heal them).

**Your saves.** Saves from 0.2.0 and 0.1.x carry over; the Labyrinth starts empty.

**Known issues**
- In co-op, the joining game can close with an error code (134) on exit. Your progress is already saved.
- The first Labyrinth floor can hitch once while its shaders compile.
