The Labyrinth goes on. Act II, the Blightcoil: one cavern you can walk, stacked around
the Rotheart.

**What's new**
- The Blightcoil: the Sump, the Canopy Ring, and the Crown.
- Waystones as checkpoints.
- Earth and Dark loot.
- Vines that slow you. They do not hurt.
- The Blightmaw Matriarch, fought in three phases.
- The Verdant Mother, and Joe's marks.
- After the fight: an empty torn cocoon, and a door down to the Forge. That door does
  not open Act III yet.
- Ember Art for the cavern and its creatures.

**Also in this build.** Since 0.3.1 the campaign is closed levels in a line: Old Oak,
town, the Landfill, the Breach, then the Labyrinth. A 0.6.0 pass cleaned up travel rows
and gate votes.

**Your saves.** Saves still load (save version 1). Act II keeps its progress in the
Labyrinth block you already have.

**Known issues**
- The Blightcoil music cues are silent.
- The Forge door does not open the next act.
- The Matriarch has very little health (authored at 120), and her Death animation does
  not play out.
- In outdoor co-op, the joining game can still close with an error code (134) on exit.
  Your progress is already saved.
- Some of the cavern is still grey boxes: slabs, the shrine, chests, and fog volumes.
