# Covenant of Embers — Staff and Wand Part Concept Art

Index: [docs/concepts/](../README.md). These paintings do not replace the in-game kit.

Painterly concept sheets for every staff and wand part combination in Covenant of Embers. Style matches the existing level and creature plates: aged cracked parchment, ornate filigree title cartouche, and dark-fantasy oil illustration.

62 JPEG sheets live in this folder, each 1280×720 and under 2 MB.

## Naming

```
<form>_<parttype>_<tier>.jpg
overview_<form>.jpg
```

All names are lowercase. Examples: `staff_exterior_rare.jpg`, `wand_handle_unique.jpg`, `overview_staff.jpg`.

- **form:** `staff` (long) or `wand` (short)
- **parttype:** `head`, `exterior`, `interior`, `handle`, `charm`
- **tier:** `basic`, `uncommon`, `rare`, `mythic`, `primordial`, `unique`

## Sheet layout

Each part sheet shows that one part type, rarity tier, and form in all seven element variants, left to right, each labeled:

Arcane, Fire, Water, Earth, Air, Light, Dark

Title in the cartouche follows the pattern `Name Type - Tier - Form`, for example `Ironwood Exterior - Rare - Staff`.

The two overview sheets show a complete assembled weapon with all five sockets called out: HEAD, CHARM, EXTERIOR, INTERIOR, HANDLE.

## Part types

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Head | Prongs or a crescent around a focus gem, with 1–3 gem sockets |
| Exterior | Shell bands along the shaft, trimmed in the rarity color |
| Interior | An emissive element core seen through a slatted cage |
| Handle | Grip wrap plus pommel at the back end |
| Charm | A small bauble hanging under the head |

## Rarity looks

Higher tiers are visibly more ornate.

| Tier | Head | Exterior | Interior | Handle | Charm |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Basic | Rough | Oak | Mana Core | Leather | Charm |
| Uncommon | Polished | Ash | Arcane Conduit | Silk | Talisman |
| Rare | Brilliant | Ironwood | Ley Channel | Chain | Sigil |
| Mythic | Ethereal | Darkwood | Void Heart | Rune-etched | Ward |
| Primordial | Ancient | Starwood | Prism Core | Crystalweave | Relic |
| Unique | Legendary | Worldtree | Soul Engine | Dragonhide | Artifact |

## Elements

Consistent color language across every sheet:

- **Arcane** — violet / purple
- **Fire** — ember orange and crimson
- **Water** — deep teal and sea blue
- **Earth** — moss green and amber
- **Air** — pale silver-cyan
- **Light** — warm gold and white
- **Dark** — void black and bruised indigo
