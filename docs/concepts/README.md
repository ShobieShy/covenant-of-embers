# Concept art

Reference paintings. They do not change a mesh, a hitbox, or which parts a weapon accepts.

| Folder | What it is |
|---|---|
| [weapons/](weapons/README.md) | Staff and wand parts. Sixty part sheets and two assembled overviews. Each part sheet shows one form, one part, and one tier in all seven elements. |

The in-game staff and wand stay the procedural kit. These paintings are the look for a later art pass, not a model swap.
