# Staff and wand parts (0.15.0)

Spec taken from the 62 sheets in `docs/concepts/weapons/`. The sheets stay the reference. This file records the silhouettes, sockets, and materials the meshes have to hit. It does not add names, open the Core, turn music on, or move the save version.

## What ships

Thirty project-made meshes, five part types by six tiers. The same mesh is used on the staff and the wand. The staff is assembled at 1.82 m (pommel tip to crescent tip). The wand is that assembly scaled to 0.40 m. Both lengths come from the overview sheets (`overview_staff.jpg` is 1.8 m beside a standing figure; `overview_wand.jpg` is 0.4 m in a hand).

Each mesh is a glTF binary under `assets/original/weapons/staff_kit/`, generated by `tools/make_weapon_parts.py`. No Godot `PrimitiveMesh` and no grey box. The pack is one row in `ASSETS.toml` and `CREDITS.md`.

A full viewmodel stays under the existing 4 000 triangle budget. Stats, brands, buffs, debuffs, part rolls, and save version 1 are unchanged. The viewmodel focus gem stays the element-table colour, unshaded, so a cast and the gem test stay one colour. The sculpted sockets, cores, veins, and stains use the sheet palette below.

## Sheet palette

Taken from the cartouches and the seven swatches on every part sheet. Arcane on the sheet is violet. The element table's arcane swatch is grey, and that grey remains the focus gem and the bolt. Everything else on the weapon uses the sheet colour.

| Element | Sheet colour | Where it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Arcane | violet (0.58, 0.22, 0.82) | gems, core, vein, stitch, mythic wood |
| Fire | ember (0.95, 0.28, 0.06) | same |
| Water | teal-blue (0.10, 0.45, 0.78) | same |
| Earth | moss (0.32, 0.52, 0.18) | same |
| Air | silver-cyan (0.72, 0.88, 0.95) | same |
| Light | warm gold (1.00, 0.78, 0.35) | same |
| Dark | bruised indigo (0.22, 0.08, 0.42) | same |

Materials live in `resources/weapons/` as `StandardMaterial3D` resources: one gem material per element, plus wood, metal, and wrap bases. `StaffKit` tints metal and wrap with the part's element and a little of the rarity colour. Mythic wood also takes the element, matching the ethereal sheets.

## Sockets and assembly

Parts stack along the shaft and overlap by about 4 cm on the 1.82 m staff so a side view has no gap. The wand uses the same overlaps, scaled.

| Part | Span on the 1.82 m staff | Reads as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handle | butt, including the pommel | grip and pommel |
| Exterior | the long shaft | carved or bound shell |
| Interior | a bulge in the upper half of the shaft | cage and core, wider than the shaft so the core covers the wood from the front |
| Head | the tip | collar, crescent, sockets |
| Charm | hangs from the collar, off to the side | pendant |

The charm and any head chains hang straight down (gravity), on the upright sheet and on the horizontal viewmodel.

Gem sockets are part of the head mesh and are always drawn, filled with the part's element. Counts follow the sheets, not a new stat:

| Tier | Sculpted sockets |
| --- | --- |
| Basic | 1, cradled in the crescent |
| Uncommon | 2, large and small |
| Rare | 3, one large and two small |
| Mythic | 1, in a thin crescent |
| Primordial | 5, a column inside the horn |
| Unique | 4 on the head, plus three small drops on chains |

`gem_slots` on the part resource is untouched. Socketed gameplay gems recolour the first sculpted sockets when they are present.

The viewmodel keeps its pose, its layer, and a cast point just past the tip. Bare hands still show a plain oak shaft and the focus gem.

## Part silhouettes

Front view, the way the sheets are drawn. Higher tiers add metal and filigree. They do not swap the crescent, the cage, or the pendant for a different object.

### Head

One horn rooted in a wrapped collar, sweeping up and over a marquise gem. The open side faces lower right.

- **Basic, Rough.** Bark horn, vine-wrapped collar, one gem.
- **Uncommon, Polished.** Smoother horn, carved collar ring, large gem plus a smaller one below it.
- **Rare, Brilliant.** Metal filigree along the horn, three gems, banded collar.
- **Mythic, Ethereal.** Thin horn, short side tines, one gem, wood stained with the element.
- **Primordial, Ancient.** Thick horn, five gems stacked in the cradle, a small rune on the stub.
- **Unique, Legendary.** Feathered outer edge, wing ribbons, central gem, side gems, three short chains with drops.

### Exterior

A straight shaft with a cap at each end.

- **Basic, Oak.** Bark, two carved bands, a cord crossed on the front, element stain in the middle.
- **Uncommon, Ash.** Pale smooth grain, a pair of thin bands, a short element inlay between them.
- **Rare, Ironwood.** Dark wood, helical filigree, an element vein, metal caps.
- **Mythic, Darkwood.** Near-black wood, denser filigree, a brighter vein. The wood takes the element.
- **Primordial, Starwood.** Warm wood, four small star gems along the front, crystal caps.
- **Unique, Worldtree.** Two vines with leaves, metal caps, element light in the grain.

### Interior

A spindle cage with wood or metal caps and a glowing core. The cage is wider than the shaft.

- **Basic, Mana Core.** Round bars, two rings, an oval core.
- **Uncommon, Arcane Conduit.** More bars, three rings, a slimmer core, metal caps.
- **Rare, Ley Channel.** Bars that bow out into leaves, a bright core, filigree caps.
- **Mythic, Void Heart.** A heart-shaped core, vine bars.
- **Primordial, Prism Core.** A hexagonal prism core, fewer bars, crystal caps.
- **Unique, Soul Engine.** A pointed window frame, two gears, a bright core.

### Handle

Grip plus a pommel. The top is a short wood stub that slides into the exterior.

- **Basic, Leather.** Cross-stitch, a bulb pommel, one diamond gem.
- **Uncommon, Silk.** Smooth wrap, a slim pommel, a small gem.
- **Rare, Chain.** Woven links in the middle, filigree above and below, a fluted pommel.
- **Mythic, Rune-etched.** The same wrap with small rune plaques. The etch takes the element.
- **Primordial, Crystalweave.** Filigree, a woven mid band, a faceted pommel.
- **Unique, Dragonhide.** Scale braid and a dragon head for the pommel: snout down, two horns, two eyes.

### Charm

The loop is the origin. The body hangs below it.

- **Basic, Charm.** Rope loop, wood teardrop, one large gem.
- **Uncommon, Talisman.** Metal bail, smoother teardrop, one gem.
- **Rare, Sigil.** Three chain links, a round medallion, a four-point gem.
- **Mythic, Ward.** Chain, an almond frame with points at the top and bottom, a four-point gem.
- **Primordial, Relic.** Heavier chain, a thick ring, a large gem.
- **Unique, Artifact.** Ornate ring, a central diamond, a small gem above it, three hanging drops.

## What this pass does not change

- Part ids, display names, roll tables, damage, cooldown, accuracy, brands, buffs, debuffs.
- Save version (`SaveGame.VERSION` stays 1) and old saves.
- Music. It stays silent.
- The Core. It stays closed.
- `project.godot` `config/version`. This is not a 1.0 bump and not a release cut.
- Levels, bosses, enemies, and UI. Those are other phases.

## Done when

Every part type and tier has a mesh. Each one renders on the staff and on the wand, sockets filled, neighbours overlapping. Contact sheets place the render over the concept sheet. The silhouette, the socket count, and the palette match the sheet. The staff kit tests pass, including the triangle cap and the meeting parts.
